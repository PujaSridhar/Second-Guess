What the baseline missed. Before three corrections were written to Cognee's permanent graph, the same corpus produced twice as many: a hedged "I'll take a look at that paper when I get a chance", a W-9 already confirmed received three lines further down its own thread, and two of Raj's action items read as Puja's.

Who decides what. The model proposes commitments and names external entities. Every verdict is plain Python — calendar collision, closure detection, deduplication, and an evidence gate that refuses BROKEN without a dated, citable source. Scores are computed against a labelled set; the model never scores itself.

Honest limits. The corpus is seeded and the internal conflicts are planted. The external facts are real and were verified live at run time. F1 of 1.00 is five commitments against ten decoys, not a benchmark.
